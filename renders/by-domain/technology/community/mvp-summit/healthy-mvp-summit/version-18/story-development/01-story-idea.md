## Unique Narrative Concept

This story uses a **MANGA MARTIAL ARTS / SENSEI-STUDENT** framework. MARCO is a young tech warrior who has always struggled at summits - burning out, crashing, losing focus. He discovers that ANDREAS has been approaching the summit as a form of discipline and practice for years. ANDREAS becomes the SENSEI, teaching MARCO the Six Paths of Summit Mastery through manga-style training sequences.

**The Hook**: Marco arrives at the summit ready to "conquer it" with maximum effort and aggression - the way he approaches everything. His sensei Andreas appears and offers a different path: mastery through balance, not force.

## Character Specifications

### Andreas Erben (The Sensei)
- **Visual**: Caucasian male late 40s early 50s, PROMINENT RECTANGULAR DARK-FRAMED GLASSES WITH THICK BLACK PLASTIC FRAMES (most distinctive feature), short thinning sandy-blonde/light brown hair with M-SHAPED RECEDING HAIRLINE thin on top, clean-shaven
- **Manga Style Attire**: His usual charcoal quilted vest over light blue button-down BUT in manga style it is drawn with dramatic sharp lines suggesting inner stillness and mastery
- **Manga Visual Language**: Often shown with calm aura, wise small smile, dramatic "sensei" framing

### Marco Casalaina (The Student)
- **Visual**: COMPLETELY BALD with TOTALLY SMOOTH SHAVED HEAD (most distinctive feature - zero hair), NO glasses, clean-shaven, slightly rounded oval face, blue-grey eyes
- **Manga Style**: Drawn with dynamic energy lines, exaggerated shock expressions for discoveries, determination poses

## Visual Style Notes

### Manga Conventions to Use
- Black and white line art with dramatic speed lines (hachure lines suggesting motion/impact)
- Large expressive eyes and emotional exaggeration in expressions
- Screen tone (dot pattern) shading for manga aesthetic
- Dramatic panel sizes - tiny panels for fast moments, huge splash panels for revelations
- "Ki" or energy aura shown as visual effects around characters
- Classic manga speech bubbles and sound effects in English but drawn in manga style
- Dramatic "chapter title" cards for each Path
