# Healthy MVP Summit V21 - Story Idea

## Title: "Summit: Press Start - A Wellness RPG"

## Version: 21.0
## Style: Retro Video Game / 16-bit RPG Pixel Art Style (SNES-era JRPG aesthetic, health bars, inventory menus, boss battles)
## Target: 35+ panels

---

## Unique Narrative Concept

This story uses an **RPG VIDEO GAME** framework. The summit is rendered as a JRPG adventure. Marco is the player character (HERO) with health points, mana for cognitive energy, and an inventory of health tools. Andreas is the WISE GUIDE (Gandalf-figure). Health threats are BOSS MONSTERS to defeat with wellness strategies.

**The Hook**: A pixel-art "game title screen" opens with "SUMMIT: PRESS START" and a cursor blinking over "NEW GAME".

---

## Visual Style Notes

### RPG Game UI Elements
- Health bars, MP/Mana bars, EXP meters visible
- Battle screens with character sprites facing monsters
- Inventory menus showing health items  
- Map screens of the campus as dungeon
- Dialogue boxes in classic RPG style
- Pixel font throughout
- 16-bit color palette
