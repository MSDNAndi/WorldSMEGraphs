# Healthy MVP Summit V20 - Story Idea

## Title: "The Summit Strip: Daily Comics from the Conference Floor"

## Version: 20.0  
## Style: Classic Newspaper Comic Strip (Peanuts/Calvin and Hobbes inspired, black and white with Sunday full-color strips)
## Target: 30+ strips/panels

---

## Unique Narrative Concept

This story is structured as a series of **NEWSPAPER COMIC STRIPS** - short 3-4 panel strips in the classic format (setup → complication → punchline). Each strip covers one health lesson in the tradition of great newspaper comics: simple, sharp, funny, memorable.

**The Hook**: "The Summit Strip - Running daily during every Microsoft MVP Summit since... well, since right now."

---

## Visual Style Notes

### Newspaper Comic Strip Conventions  
- 3-4 panels per strip, horizontal layout
- Simple clean line art, minimal backgrounds
- Classic character designs with consistent visual shorthand
- Punchy dialogue and visual gags
- Black and white with selective color in Sunday strips
- Classic comic strip panel borders
