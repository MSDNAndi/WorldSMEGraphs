# Healthy MVP Summit V19 - Story Idea

## Title: "Summit in Watercolor: A Day of Mindful Presence"

## Version: 19.0
## Style: Impressionistic Watercolor Illustration (soft edges, wet-on-wet color bleeding, translucent washes, painterly aesthetic)
## Target: 30+ panels

---

## Unique Narrative Concept

This story uses a **MINDFULNESS / IMPRESSIONISTIC** framework. Unlike the action-focused previous versions, this is a quiet, contemplative story. Andreas guides Marco through experiencing the summit as a mindful practice rather than a conquest. The watercolor art style reflects the inner experience of mindfulness - soft edges, flowing colors, moments of beauty found in ordinary conference moments.

**The Hook**: Marco arrives overwhelmed and anxious. Andreas offers him a different lens: instead of a battlefield to conquer, the summit is a gallery to explore mindfully.

---

## Visual Style Notes

### Watercolor Conventions
- Soft wet-on-wet color bleeding between elements
- Translucent color washes that layer and build
- Loose gestural linework rather than tight comic book borders
- White space used deliberately for breathing room  
- Colors shift with mood: anxious scenes in cool blues, mindful moments in warm golds
- Text in beautiful calligraphic style rather than bold comic fonts
