# Healthy MVP Summit V22 - Story Idea

## Title: "Summit Blueprint: Engineering Your Perfect Conference"

## Version: 22.0
## Style: Technical Blueprint / Engineering Diagram Style (white lines on blueprint blue, technical specifications, engineering drawing aesthetic)
## Target: 30+ panels

---

## Unique Narrative Concept

This story uses an **ENGINEERING BLUEPRINT** framework. The summit survival guide presented as a technical document - complete with engineering specifications, system diagrams, safety requirements, and technical drawings. Andreas is the SENIOR ENGINEER reviewing Marco's faulty prototype summit plan and showing him the correct specifications.

**The Hook**: Marco's "SUMMIT ATTENDANCE PLAN v1.0" blueprint is reviewed by Senior Engineer Andreas and rejected as having critical design flaws.

---

## Visual Style Notes

### Blueprint Aesthetics
- White technical linework on blueprint blue backgrounds
- Engineering drawing conventions: orthographic projections, callouts, dimension lines
- Technical specification tables
- System flow diagrams
- Gear and machinery metaphors for biological processes
- Professional engineering document headers with revision numbers
